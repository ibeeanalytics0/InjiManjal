The Name That Tells the Story

Every word in our brand name carries the weight of generations. Inji — ginger — is the first healer a Tamil grandmother reaches for. Manjal — turmeric — is the golden ingredient in Tamil homes, food, and rituals for over 4,000 years.

Together, they don't just represent products. They represent a philosophy: that ancient wisdom about growing and eating clean food is not a premium lifestyle choice. It is simply the way food should be.
